# FIFO Costing

Primary costing method: **cost layers** created on receipt; outbound consumes oldest `received_at` first.

## Outcomes

- Every [[Stock Movement]] outbound gets unit/total cost
- Inventory valuation = Σ(layer qty_remaining × unit_cost)
- COGS for issues/sales
- Landed cost can increase layer costs
